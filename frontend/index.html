By substantially reducing the proportion of youth not in employment, education, or training (NEET). CareerPath AI provides free, democratized career pathways to bridge college curricula with modern tech industry hiring criteria.

Empowering students from tier-2 and tier-3 colleges, self-taught coders, and career switchers to acquire high-value software engineering skills without expensive bootcamps or barriers.
